About RideX Motors

Sakhipur's premier destination for quality verified pre-owned motorcycles, genuine paperwork, and trustworthy motorbike trade-in solutions.

TRUST & TRANSPARENCY

Setting the Benchmark for Used Bikes in Tangail

Founded with a passion for quality two-wheelers, RideX Motors is situated in Kachua Road, Sakhipur, Tangail. Buying a second-hand motorbike can often be filled with uncertainties regarding mechanical health and legal paperwork. We built RideX Motors to completely eliminate those risks.

Every bike that enters our showroom undergoes a strict multi-point inspection — checking engine compression, chassis alignment, electrical systems, and tire wear. Furthermore, we verify all legal papers from the Bangladesh Road Transport Authority (BRTA) to ensure a 100% dispute-free ownership transition.

COMPLETE RIDER CARE

Original Motul Engine Oil, Helmets & Accessories

Beyond motorcycle sales and trade-ins, we take pride in offering genuine care products for your bike. In our showroom shelves, you will find 100% authentic Motul 7100 and 3100 4T engine oils, high-grade WD-40, chain lubricants, and certified safety helmets.

When you purchase a bike from RideX Motors, our technician guides you on the optimal maintenance schedule, recommended engine oil grades, and tire maintenance to ensure your motorcycle performs at its peak for years to come.
